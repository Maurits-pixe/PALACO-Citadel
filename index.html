RIO Platform Ascension

RIO evolves from chat interface to PALACO conversation and communication platform.

Surface-independent conversation

  • One conversation identity across mobile, web, Citadel, ELIXER, and desktop.
  • Conversation belongs to PALACO context, not to one device.
  • Cross-surface continuity remains active through sync and reconnect states.

Presence with boundaries

  • Presence means reachable, never automatic authority.
  • Online ≠ authorized; constitutional gates remain separate.
  • RIO connects communication while PALACO protects authority boundaries.

Provenance-aware communication

  • Messages preserve identity, context, origin, time, and traceability.
  • WATERMERK and HOLOGRAM support authenticity and lineage visibility.
  • Complexity is hidden when irrelevant and revealed when material.

Canonical law: RIO may connect communication; RIO shall not create authority.

RIO Fabric, Rooms, Address, Handoff

  • One communication fabric across Mobile, Web, Citadel, and ELIXER surfaces.
  • RIO Rooms are conversation contexts: joining a room never grants authority.
  • RIO Address and RIO Link route communication destinations without granting permission.
  • RIO Handshake and Trust Indicators clarify identity, context, and communication risk.
  • Conversation continuity supports handoff between surfaces with context re-check.
  • VisitCard Beacon supports discovery-first introduction with Watermerk/Hologram verification before handshake.
  • VisitCard 03 interaction flow: Discover → Recognize → Meet → Connect → Explore.

Authority boundaries

Who may do what, why, and under which boundaries.

  • Who: Holder

    What: Can execute own approved actions

    Why: Identity-bound ownership

    Boundary: Only within granted scope

    Conditions: Valid proof + active session

  • Who: Steward

    What: Can review and co-authorize sensitive actions

    Why: Governance and duty of care

    Boundary: Cannot impersonate holder authority

    Conditions: Review trail + dual confirmation

  • Who: System

    What: Can validate, log, and enforce policy gates

    Why: Constitutional integrity

    Boundary: No creation of human authority

    Conditions: Policy match + verifiable evidence
