# RIO-UNIVERSAL-001 — UNIVERSAL CONVERSATION LAYER

## Canonical direction

ONE RIO. MANY SURFACES. ONE CONVERSATIONAL CONTEXT.

RIO becomes a universal conversational entry point to PALACO across mobile, web, ELIXER, Citadel, and future supported environments.

## Universal components

- **RIO Identity**: communicative identity awareness for person, Citadel, ELIXER, or permitted system identity.
- **RIO Address**: human-readable destination resolved into a valid communication endpoint.
- **RIO Link**: opens a communication destination; does not confer permission.
- **RIO Handshake**: first-contact flow with identity/context/access checks and accept/decline control.
- **RIO Trust Indicators**: verified, insufficient verification, unverified, unknown.
- **RIO Conversation State**: discovered, requested, connected, active, paused, resumed, closed (+ disputed/conflicted/in doubt).

## Governance bindings

- Communication is not finality; event status belongs to CEFCG when relevant.
- Revoke withdraws authorization and does not erase conversation history.
- Privacy visibility must remain user-facing: who can see what, why, how long, and with whom shared.
- Progressive disclosure applies: simple outside, reconstructable depth when material.
