## Purpose
The Treaty of ATLANTIS defines bounded federative governance under the PALACO Constitution. ATLANTIS enforces constitutional meaning and shall never replace it.

## Constitutional hierarchy
PALACO CONSTITUTION > TREATY OF ATLANTIS > CODEX ATLANTIS > CONCILIE VAN ATLANTIS > FEDERATIVE GOVERNANCE > POLICY > AUTHORIZED ACTION.

## Core laws
1. Federation membership does not confer constitutional authority.
2. Worlds retain identity, culture, dialect, architecture, local governance, history, Citadel, ELIXERS and lawful relationships.
3. MANY WORLDS. MANY DIALECTS. ONE CONSTITUTION.
4. Constitutional thinning may simplify presentation but shall never remove constitutional meaning or protection.
5. The Concilie van ATLANTIS is the highest federative governing body within ATLANTIS and is never above the PALACO Constitution.
6. The Concilie consists of twelve appointed PALACO Intergalactic Raadgevers. Council membership does not create personal sovereignty or unbounded authority.
7. PACHT is an evidence-bound, scope-bound, version-bound, time-bound, reviewable and revocable PALACO authorized quality label.
8. PACHT is not sovereignty, ownership, popularity, general endorsement, permanent approval or constitutional authority.
9. Majority does not create constitutional validity. Invalid evidence cannot be repaired by voting.
10. WATERMERK and HOLOGRAM support provenance and authenticity; neither creates authority.
11. REVOKE preserves history. REVOKED does not mean NEVER EXISTED.
12. Normative evolution occurs only through a controlled ∆ transition with traceable succession.
13. Human agency shall be preserved. No federation process, interface, translation, thinning, Council procedure or PACHT mechanism may replace attributable human choice, infer consent, or remove a lawful human exit or review path.
14. Dissent shall remain attributable, evidence-bound and preserved in the decision record; dissent may not be silently erased by majority, authorization, challenge resolution or later succession.

## Semantic integrity
World dialects may be translated, contextualized and optimally thinned only when constitutional meaning is preserved. Ambiguous or non-equivalent constitutional mappings fail closed.
